Shipping Information

Delivery Timelines

We process all orders within 24 hours of placement. Standard nationwide delivery across Pakistan takes 3 to 5 business days from the time your order is dispatched.

Shipping Charges

  • Orders under Rs. 5,000: A standard flat shipping rate of Rs. 200 applies to all orders.
  • Orders Rs. 5,000 and above: Enjoy Free Shipping on us. This discount is automatically applied at checkout.

Payment Methods

We currently offer Cash on Delivery (COD) nationwide so you can securely pay for your order when it arrives at your doorstep.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched from our facility, you will receive an SMS or email from our courier partner with your tracking number. You can use this tracking number to check the live status of your delivery.
